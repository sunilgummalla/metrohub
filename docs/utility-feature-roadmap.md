# MetroHub Utility Feature Roadmap

Based on the strong engagement with social gaming utilities (Scorecards, Tambola, Bingo) and the goal to drive impulse buying for physical snacks, event tickets, RSVPs, and social gatherings, the strategy should focus on tools that seamlessly blend event organization with immediate purchasing opportunities.

The following roadmap outlines high-impact utilities designed to keep users engaged and naturally funnel them toward transactions.

## Phase 1: Core Gathering & Social Tools

These tools build directly on the existing user base that utilizes MetroHub for game nights and gatherings.

### 1. The "Game Night" RSVP & Potluck Planner (`app-game-night`)
A dedicated utility for organizing the logistics of the game nights where users are already using the Scorecards.

*   **Utility Value:** Allows a host to create an event, send RSVP links, and set up a potluck sign-up sheet. It removes the friction of coordinating via WhatsApp or text.
*   **Impulse Buying Integration:**
    *   **Snack Bundles:** When a guest signs up for the potluck, offer a "Skip cooking, bring the party" button. This links directly to physical snack bundles or party platters available on the portal.
    *   **Host Gifts:** Suggest small impulse buys for guests to bring to the host.

### 2. Event Ticketing & RSVP Manager (`app-events`)
A centralized place to discover, RSVP, and buy tickets for local community events, building on the "Near By" concept.

*   **Utility Value:** Users can browse local events, RSVP for free gatherings, or purchase tickets for paid events.
*   **Impulse Buying Integration:**
    *   **Ticket Sales:** Direct revenue from selling event tickets.
    *   **Add-ons:** When buying a ticket or RSVPing, offer add-ons like "Pre-order your snack pack" or "Reserve premium seating."

## Phase 2: Gamification & Engagement Retention

These features aim to increase daily active users (DAU) and create a habit of checking the portal.

### 3. Community Leaderboards & Tournaments (`app-tournaments`)
Extend the current scorecards by allowing users to create ongoing leagues or tournaments for Poker, Rummy, or other games.

*   **Utility Value:** Keeps players coming back to check standings and log new scores over weeks or months.
*   **Impulse Buying Integration:**
    *   **Prize Pools:** Allow the group to pool money for a prize, which can be redeemed for portal credit or specific high-value items.
    *   **Sponsorships:** Promote specific local services or products as the "Sponsor of the Week" on the leaderboard page.

### 4. The "Snack Roulette" / Decision Maker (`app-decider`)
A fun, lightweight utility for groups trying to decide what to eat or do.

*   **Utility Value:** A simple spinner or randomizer that helps a group make a decision.
*   **Impulse Buying Integration:**
    *   **Curated Options:** Ensure that options available for immediate purchase on the portal (e.g., specific snack packs or local services) are heavily featured or guaranteed to win occasionally, prompting an immediate buy.

## Phase 3: Financial & Shared Expenses

Building on the "Splits" and "My Accounts" infrastructure to facilitate group purchasing.

### 5. Group Purchasing & Gift Pooling (`app-group-buy`)
A utility that allows a group to easily collect funds for a shared purchase.

*   **Utility Value:** Simplifies the awkward process of collecting money for a birthday gift, a shared event ticket, or a large party order.
*   **Impulse Buying Integration:**
    *   **Direct Fulfillment:** Once the financial goal is reached, the funds can be immediately used to purchase the item or service directly from the portal, keeping the transaction entirely within the ecosystem.

## Strategic Recommendations for Implementation

1.  **Seamless Integration:** The transition from utility to purchase must be frictionless. For example, the Scorecard app should have a subtle "Refill Snacks" button that opens a quick-buy modal without leaving the game screen.
2.  **Contextual Offers:** Tailor the impulse buys to the context. A Poker night might prompt for chips and drinks, while a Tambola event might prompt for tea and biscuits.
3.  **Leverage Existing Apps:** Before building entirely new apps, consider adding the impulse buy hooks into the existing `app-rummy-scorecard`, `app-poker-scorecard`, and `app-tambola`.

By focusing on utilities that solve real coordination problems for social gatherings, MetroHub can naturally introduce relevant products and services precisely when the users need them most.
